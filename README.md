# Mumbai-Real-Estate-Analysis-House-Price-Tracking-
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of Mumbai's real estate market by tracking house prices, property availability, regional distribution, and other important housing metrics. It helps users understand price trends, compare different property types, and identify areas with higher or lower property values.
1. High-Level Purpose
Core Business Question

How are house prices distributed across Mumbai, and what factors (property type, location, affordability tier, rental market, and under-construction projects) influence property values to support buying, selling, and investment decisions?

This dashboard provides a 360-degree view of Mumbai's real estate market by combining:

Property sale prices
Rental market analysis
Regional price distribution
Property type comparisons
Under-construction project insights

It helps stakeholders such as home buyers, investors, builders, and real estate analysts understand where the highest-value properties are located, how prices have changed over time, and which segments offer the best opportunities.

4. Actionable Takeaways
For Home Buyers
Compare property prices by region before making a purchase.
If budget is limited, explore Navi Mumbai or Thane instead of South Mumbai.
Use the price-per-square-foot metric to evaluate whether a property is fairly priced.
Consider average home loan rates when estimating financing costs.
For Real Estate Investors
Focus on South Mumbai and Western Mumbai for premium capital appreciation.
Analyze historical price trends to identify favorable buying periods.
Use rental metrics to identify areas with stronger rental income potential.
Monitor under-construction projects to invest before prices appreciate after completion.
For Property Developers
High demand in luxury and premium segments suggests continued opportunities for upscale developments.
Areas with increasing builder activity indicate strong future market potential.
Use possession timelines to plan new project launches and avoid oversupply.
For Rental Investors
Invest in localities with higher average monthly rents and strong rental availability.
Prioritize 2 BHK and 3 BHK properties, as they typically attract broader tenant demand and offer balanced rental returns.
Compare rent per square foot to assess income efficiency across locations.
Overall Business Conclusion

This dashboard serves as a comprehensive decision-support tool for Mumbai's real estate market. It combines property sale prices, rental performance, regional demand, historical price trends, and construction activity into a single view. Users can evaluate where property values are highest, identify the most profitable investment locations, compare property categories, and monitor future market supply. By integrating pricing, rental, and development insights, the dashboard enables informed decisions for buying, selling, investing, and planning real estate projects.
